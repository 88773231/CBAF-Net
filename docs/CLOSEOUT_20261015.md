# CCF C Manuscript and Release Closeout

## Final Method Decision

The final bounded semantic screen did not meet either complete-study
continuation rule. No development architecture is promoted. Stop further
training and data expansion. Preserve development source and negative
aggregate evidence as separate research history.

The working manuscript describes the original adapted CE-only BotDMM
backbone plus independently fitted HistGradientBoosting behavioral model,
combined by a validation-selected global probability weight. It does
not contain four experts, a learned router, AMR parsing, pretrained
semantic embeddings, or inter-post interval features.

## Evidence and Manuscript

Quadbot, with 6,708 controlled trajectories, is primary. Twibot22, with
5,031 trajectories, is a nested auxiliary view of the same 1,677 profiles,
not the official TwiBot-22 benchmark. Accounts, labels and splits are
unchanged.

Six main runs, twelve prediction splits, six manuscript result rows,
validation-selected mixture weights, and all 34 immutable result-manifest
files passed the local alignment audit. All 242 regression tests passed.
The strict results remain in `results/strict_postprocessed/` and the
immutable `v1.1.0` tag is not replaced.

The one working manuscript is titled "CBAF Net Behavioral Statistics
Fusion for Controlled Multiclass Social Bot Detection". Its revisions
remove unsupported architecture and superiority claims, distinguish
inputs/objectives from the original BotDMM, and disclose hybrid
controller-mediated generation and retained archive limitations.
Reference [13] now identifies OASIS, arXiv:2411.11581.

The editable Figure 1 now labels the illustrated record "Action log";
the timestamp icon is not a timing-feature claim. Tables preserve all
reported numerical values and identify per-metric best means.

The manuscript and author title page remain private working artifacts,
not approved public preprints. Their exact hashes and the matched figure
and evidence hashes are pinned by the closeout manifest. Separate private
receipts record rendered page checks, remote commit/artifact equality,
dataset-master integrity and public branch synchronization. A local
manifest alone is not evidence that remote synchronization completed.

## Submission Gates

- Confirm human author contribution roles and both authors' final approval.
- Resolve source access/transformation/distribution permission and the
  institutional ethics determination; see `SOURCE_PERMISSIONS.md`.
- Select a current scope-compatible CCF-C venue recognized by the authors'
  institution, including any separate SCIE requirement.
- Apply that venue's anonymity, reference, length and template rules.
  Removing author names alone does not anonymize public GitHub links.
- Verify the earlier submission is no longer under consideration.
- Obtain separate confirmation immediately before any formal submission.

No guaranteed SOTA result, helpfulness of every component, CCF-C
acceptance, legal clearance or ethics exemption is claimed. The limited
gain over the strongest standalone model and the incomplete end-to-end
generation archive remain scientific/reproducibility limitations.
Document polishing does not remove them.

## Runtime and Data Safety

This closeout involves document rendering, result recomputation and file
synchronization, not GPU training. No paid GPU is needed. No original
dataset, unique failed-run evidence, release tag, checkpoint or prediction
is deleted. Public exports omit raw text, original identities, credentials
and model weights.
