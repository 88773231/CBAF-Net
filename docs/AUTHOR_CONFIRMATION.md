# Author Confirmations Before Submission

Software checks and model scores cannot establish these declarations.
The authors must confirm the following against their actual records before a
submission-ready manuscript or cover letter is prepared.

| Item | Required evidence | Current boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Authorship and order | Both authors approve their contribution descriptions and the final manuscript | Do not infer contributions from affiliation or corresponding-author status |
| Corresponding author | Zhang Bin approves the role and contact information | Account/submission actions do not establish approval of a revised scientific claim |
| Source permission | Exact TwiBot-22 source terms and access procedure; permitted derived uses | The code's MIT license does not grant source-data redistribution rights |
| Generator records | Retained checkpoint identity, decoding configuration, prompts, simulator configuration and source licenses | One-generator traces cannot establish cross-generator robustness |
| Ethics and privacy | Applicable institutional approval, exemption or substantiated explanation | Do not invent an approval number or claim unrestricted data consent |
| Funding and conflicts | Authors reconfirm no funding and no competing interests | Earlier user declarations can be carried forward only if still accurate |
| Reproduction scope | Identify public code/results, restricted inputs and unavailable artifacts separately | No public end-to-end regeneration claim without the missing components |
| Submission status | Previous manuscript rejected/withdrawn and not concurrently under review elsewhere | Do not create a duplicate concurrent submission |

No researcher name, approval, license, ethics exemption, or declaration should be
fabricated to complete this checklist. Outstanding fields remain outstanding.
